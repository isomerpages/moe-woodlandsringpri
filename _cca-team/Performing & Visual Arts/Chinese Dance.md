<h2>Overview:</h2>
<p>Chinese Dance&nbsp;consists of many modern and traditional dance genres.
The dances cover a wide range, from folk dances to performances in opera
and ballet, and may be used in public celebrations, rituals and ceremonies.
The best known Chinese dances today are the&nbsp;Dragon dance and the&nbsp;Lion
Dance.</p>
<p>Currently, the Chinese dancers in our school perform at various school
events like Chinese New Year Concert, National Day Celebration and other
major school events etc. They also participate in the Central SYF Judging
held every two years. The Chinese dancers also have the opportunity to
showcase their dance moves through performances outside school, like the
Woodlands Community Chinese New Year Celebration etc.</p>
