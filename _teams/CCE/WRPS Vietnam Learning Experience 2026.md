<h4><strong>5D 4N OVERSEAS LEARNING EXPERIENCE (SELECTED P5 STUDENTS) TO HANOI, VIETNAM&nbsp;&nbsp;</strong></h4>
<p><strong>SATURDAY,&nbsp;25 April 2026 to WEDNESDAY,&nbsp;29 April 2026&nbsp;</strong>
</p>
<p>Selected Primary Five students travelled to Hanoi to learn more about
Vietnamese culture, history, and daily life through hands-on experiences.
During the trip, they built teamwork and independence while exploring historical
landmarks, tasting local food, and interacting with local communities.
The journey also helped them develop confidence, friendships, and a deeper
appreciation for different cultures.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Vạn Phúc Silk Village</strong>
</p>
<p>The students visited Vạn Phúc Silk Village to learn about Vietnam’s traditional
silk-making industry and cultural heritage. They observed how skilled artisans
weave silk by hand, explored local shops, and discovered the importance
of preserving traditional crafts. The visit helped students appreciate
craftsmanship, history, and the value of supporting local communities and
traditions.</p>

<p><strong>Bat Trang Ceramic Village</strong>
</p>
<p>The students visited Bát Tràng Ceramic Village to explore Vietnam’s famous
pottery-making traditions and learn about local craftsmanship. They watched
artisans shape and decorate ceramics, discovered the history of the village,
and experienced hands-on pottery activities. The visit encouraged creativity,
cultural appreciation, and a better understanding of Vietnam’s rich artistic
heritage.</p>

<p><strong>Hong Ngoc or Dai Viet Fine Art. (Vietnamese Egg Shell Lacquerware Gallery)</strong>
</p>
<p>The students visited Hong Ngoc Fine Art and Dai Viet Fine Art to learn
about Vietnamese eggshell lacquerware, a unique traditional art form. They
observed intricate techniques used by artists, explored detailed artworks,
and discovered the cultural significance behind lacquer painting. The visit
inspired creativity and deepened their appreciation for Vietnam’s artistic
heritage and craftsmanship.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>BAN MAI SCHOOL</strong>
</p>
<p>The students visited Ban Mai School to interact with local students and
experience school life in Vietnam. Through cultural exchange activities,
games, and group discussions, they built friendships and learned about
different educational practices. The visit encouraged communication, teamwork,
and mutual understanding while helping students appreciate cultural diversity
and global connections.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>VIETNAM-AUSTRALIA SCHOOL (VAS)</strong>
</p>
<p>The students visited Vietnam-Australia School (Hanoi) to engage with local
students and learn about education in Vietnam. Through interactive activities,
cultural exchanges, and collaborative learning experiences, they developed
communication and teamwork skills. The visit also helped students build
friendships, gain global awareness, and appreciate similarities and differences
between schools in Singapore and Vietnam.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>SIGHT-SEEING (CULTURAL)</strong>
</p>
<p>The students took part in cultural sightseeing around Hanoi to explore
Vietnam’s rich history, traditions, and famous landmarks. By visiting historical
sites, local markets, and cultural attractions, they gained a deeper understanding
of Vietnamese heritage and daily life. The experience broadened their perspectives,
encouraged curiosity, and strengthened their appreciation for different
cultures and communities.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>FOOD</strong>
</p>
<p>The students experienced Vietnamese food culture in Hanoi by tasting traditional
dishes and learning about local dining customs. Through trying different
foods, they discovered unique flavours, ingredients, and cooking styles
that reflect Vietnam’s rich heritage. The experience encouraged openness
to new experiences and helped students appreciate cultural diversity through
food and shared meals.</p>
