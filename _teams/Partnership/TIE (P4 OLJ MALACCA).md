<h4><strong>2D 1N MALACCA, MALAYSIA</strong></h4>
<p>The Primary Four students visited Malacca as the inaugural Tier 1 plan
for the WRPS TIE programme, involving the entire cohort. For many students,
it was their first time travelling away from their parents. The trip provided
meaningful exposure to cultural and historical learning through heritage
sites and local experiences, while building independence, resilience, teamwork,
and a deeper appreciation of Southeast Asian culture.</p>
